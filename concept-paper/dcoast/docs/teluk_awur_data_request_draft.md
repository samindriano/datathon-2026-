Dear Dr. Lilik Maslukah,

My name is Samuel Indriano. I am developing D'Coast, a student concept for
satellite-assisted coastal-water screening, and I am studying your Teluk Awur
work:

- Sabila et al. (2024), *A Robust Algorithm for Estimating Total Suspended
  Solids (TSS) Using Sentinel-2*; and
- Sabila et al. (2025), *Pollution Index Mapping Based on Total Suspended
  Solids Concentration Extracted From Sentinel-2 MSI Imagery*.

The papers provide a strong technical basis for a reproducibility benchmark.
Would it be possible to obtain the row-level data supporting the reported
110/112 samples, subject to your preferred terms?

The most useful fields would be:

- sample/station identifier and latitude/longitude with CRS;
- sampling date, local time, timezone, and sampling depth;
- in-situ TSS in mg/L and laboratory method;
- matched Sentinel-2 product identifier and B2-B8 reflectance values;
- tuning/validation split and any quality or exclusion flags; and
- processing notes for atmospheric correction, pixel extraction, and
  reflectance scaling.

I would also appreciate clarification on the reported counts of 110 in-situ
stations versus 112 reflectance samples, and 54 versus 55 observations in the
validation summary.

The data would first be used only to reproduce the published method. I will
follow any citation, confidentiality, redistribution, or derived-output
conditions you specify, and I will not redistribute the raw table without
permission.

Thank you for considering this request.

Samuel Indriano
